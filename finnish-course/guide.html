Your first hour in Finland

Just landed at Helsinki Airport? Here's the Finnish you'll actually use between the gate and the city.

~10 minutes · zero Finnish needed

In about 10 minutes you'll be able to:

  1. Say hello, good morning, good evening and good night the way locals do.
  2. Get from the airport to Helsinki Central by train, bus or taxi.
  3. Buy a bottle of water and get through the checkout.

Almost everyone in Helsinki speaks English. Starting in Finnish still earns you a smile.

Start

1

Say hello like a local

Finns keep greetings short. One word is usually enough.

Quick check

It's 8 pm. You walk into a restaurant. What do you say?

  • Huomenta!
  • Hyvää iltaa!
  • Öitä!
Show answer

Hyvää iltaa is a friendly, polite 'good evening'. Öitä is what you say when you leave late at night.

2

Get into the city

The airport is about 20 km north of Helsinki. You have three good options.

Train (juna) Recommended

Time
about 30 min
Price
single ticket, zones ABC, about €4–5 check
Where
follow the Juna / Trains signs down to the airport station under the terminal.
Which train
both the I and P trains go to Helsinki Central. Take whichever comes first. check
Ticket
buy in the HSL app or at the machine before you board. You can't buy one on the train.
Get off at
the end of the line, Helsinki (Helsingin päärautatieasema).

Bus (bussi)

Time
about 45–60 min check
Price
same HSL ticket, zones ABC check
Where
the bus stops outside arrivals; bus 600 check
Get off at
Rautatientori (Railway Square), next to Helsinki Central.
Good for
late at night, or when your hotel is on the bus route.

Taxi (taksi)

Time
about 30 min
Price
about €40–60 check
Where
the official taxi rank outside arrivals, or a ride app.
Tip
ask for the price or a fixed fare before you go. Card payment is normal.

Signs you'll see

  • Juna → Train
  • Raide → Track / platform
  • Lähtevät → Departures

Quick check

You're on the airport platform. Two trains arrive: I and P. Which one goes to Helsinki Central?

  • Only I
  • Only P
  • Both
Show answer

Both. The airport sits on a loop line, so either direction ends at Helsinki Central.

3

Buy a bottle of water

Your first purchase, and a good first Finnish conversation. You'll find a shop (an R-Kioski, K-Market or Alepa) at the airport and near every station.

Before you buy

  • Finnish tap water is excellent and free. A refillable bottle is all you need.
  • Many bottled waters are sparkling. For still water, look for hiilihapoton.
  • Plastic bottles carry a small deposit (pantti). Return the empty bottle to a machine in any supermarket and you get it back.

Words

  • vesi

    water

    VE-si

  • vesipullo

    water bottle

    VE-si-pul-lo

  • hiilihapoton

    still (no bubbles)

    HEE-li-ha-po-ton

  • hiilihapollinen

    sparkling

    HEE-li-ha-pol-li-nen check

  • Tämä, kiitos.

    This one, please.

    TÆ-mæ KEE-tos

Checkout simulator check

  1. CashierMoi! Hi!
    • Moi!
    • Hyvää yötä!
    • Kiitti!
  2. CashierTarvitsetko kassin? Do you need a bag?
    • Ei, kiitos. (No, thanks.)
    • Kyllä, kiitos. (Yes, please.)
  3. CashierSe on kaksi euroa. That's two euros.
    • Voinko maksaa kortilla?
    • Paljonko se maksaa?
  4. CashierKuitti? Receipt?
    • Ei, kiitos. Moi moi!
    • Huomenta!

You did it! 🎉 First purchase in Finnish.

Quick check

You want still water. Which word do you look for on the label?

  • hiilihapoton
  • hiilihapollinen
  • pantti
Show answer

Hiilihapoton = no bubbles. (Pantti is the bottle deposit.)
